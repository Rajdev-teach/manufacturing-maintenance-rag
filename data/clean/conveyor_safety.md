# Conveyor Operation and Safety

## Before startup
Confirm guards, pull cords, interlocks, and emergency stops are functional. Clear tools and material from the belt, verify personnel are outside hazard zones, and sound the startup warning.

## Safe operation
Never reach over, under, or into a moving conveyor. Do not clear jams or adjust tracking while equipment is energized. Loose clothing, jewelry, and long hair must be secured. Walkways and access to emergency stops must remain clear.

## Jam clearing
Stop the conveyor, follow the full lockout/tagout procedure, verify zero energy, and use tools designed for material removal. Inspect belt tracking and guarding before controlled restart. Record recurring jams as a defect for root-cause analysis.
