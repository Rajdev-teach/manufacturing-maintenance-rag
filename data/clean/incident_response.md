# Maintenance Incident Response

## Immediate actions
Stop work, make the area safe without creating additional risk, and call emergency services when needed. Provide first aid only within your training. Preserve the scene except where movement is necessary to prevent further harm.

## Reporting
Notify the supervisor promptly. Record who, what, when, where, equipment state, energy sources, witnesses, and immediate controls. Do not assign blame or speculate. Photographs and equipment logs should be retained according to company policy.

## Investigation
Use a structured method such as five whys or a cause-and-effect diagram. Identify immediate, contributing, and system causes. Corrective actions need an owner, due date, verification method, and closure evidence. Share relevant lessons with affected teams.
