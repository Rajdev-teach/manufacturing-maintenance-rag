# Lockout/Tagout Standard Operating Procedure

## Purpose
Lockout/tagout (LOTO) prevents unexpected energization or release of stored energy while equipment is serviced. Only trained, authorized employees may apply or remove locks and tags.

## Shutdown sequence
Notify affected employees, identify every energy source, and stop the machine using its normal control. Isolate electrical, hydraulic, pneumatic, thermal, gravitational, and mechanical energy. Each authorized worker applies a personal lock and completed tag to every isolation point. Release or restrain stored energy, then verify zero-energy state by attempting a normal start and testing with an appropriate meter. Return controls to off after verification.

## Return to service
Inspect the work area, reinstall guards, remove tools, and confirm employees are safely positioned. Each worker removes their own lock. Notify affected employees before re-energizing. A supervisor may use the documented absent-worker removal procedure only after verifying the worker is not on site and making reasonable contact attempts.
