# Rotating Equipment Bearing Diagnostics

## Symptoms
A high-frequency vibration increase can indicate early bearing damage. A strong one-times-running-speed peak commonly points to imbalance, while two-times-running-speed may indicate misalignment. Grinding noise, rising temperature, and metallic particles in lubricant require prompt inspection.

## Inspection
Compare readings with the asset's own baseline under the same speed and load. Check lubrication quantity and type, shaft alignment, mounting tightness, contamination, and seal condition. Do not add grease automatically: over-lubrication raises temperature and may damage seals.

## Escalation
Stop equipment immediately when vibration exceeds the alarm limit with rapid upward trend, temperature approaches the approved maximum, or noise suggests mechanical contact. Otherwise create a planned corrective work order and increase monitoring frequency.
