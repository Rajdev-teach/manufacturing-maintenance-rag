# Preventive Maintenance Program

## Schedule
Operators perform daily visual checks for leaks, loose fasteners, unusual noise, heat, vibration, and damaged guards. Maintenance technicians complete weekly lubrication and belt inspections. Monthly work includes alignment checks, filter replacement when differential pressure exceeds the limit, and verification of emergency stops. Quarterly work includes vibration trending and electrical thermography.

## Work orders
Every task must record asset ID, date, technician, meter reading, observed condition, work performed, parts used, downtime, and follow-up action. A work order is not complete until the equipment is tested and its status is updated in the computerized maintenance management system (CMMS).

## Prioritization
Safety-critical defects are priority one and require immediate isolation. Failures that stop production are priority two. Degradation with an available backup is priority three. Cosmetic issues are priority four.
